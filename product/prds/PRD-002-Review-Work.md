# PRD-002 — Review Work

## Purpose

Enable users to review employee outputs safely and approve when required.

## Users

- Attorneys
- Client Success managers

## Problem

Employee work must be reviewable, governed, and professional without requiring users to understand internal mechanics.

## Success Criteria

- Reviewers can quickly confirm whether content is appropriate.
- Approval decisions are tracked and linked to required governance.

## Scope

- Draft review screen
- Approval/deny decision UX (contract-driven; implementation later)
- Visible guardrails and professional messaging constraints

## Out of Scope

- Automated sending
- AI interaction

## Future Enhancements

- Feedback-driven improvements
- More detailed scenario explanations
