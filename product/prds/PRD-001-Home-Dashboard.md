# PRD-001 — Home Dashboard

## Purpose

Give users a clear starting point: what needs attention, what is in progress, and what completed value was created.

## Users

- Law firm owners and case managers
- Legal Ops admins

## Problem

Users lack a single, governed view into employee-driven work.

## Success Criteria

- Users can identify their next action within 30 seconds.
- Approvals and review work are understandable and auditable.

## Scope

- Navigation to Work Queue
- High-level status of the user’s team

## Out of Scope

- Execution automation
- Detailed analytics

## Future Enhancements

- Performance trends
- Department-level aggregation
