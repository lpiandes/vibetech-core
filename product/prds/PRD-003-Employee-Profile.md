# PRD-003 — Employee Profile

## Purpose

Let teams understand what a Digital Employee is, what it does, and how it behaves.

## Users

- Operations managers
- Department owners
- Attorneys / reviewers

## Problem

People need clarity: what this employee is responsible for, what actions require approval, and what outcomes it supports.

## Success Criteria

- Users can read an employee profile in under 2 minutes.
- Governance constraints (what requires approval) are obvious.

## Scope

- Employee profile page
- Business identity artifacts: mission, guardrails, decision intent, scenarios

## Out of Scope

- Running the employee
- Provider configuration

## Future Enhancements

- Marketplace browsing
- Version history of employee definitions
