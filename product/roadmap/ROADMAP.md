# Product Roadmap (Major Milestones)

This roadmap tracks product milestones only.

It is **not** an engineering task list.

## Milestone 1: Digital Employee Foundations

- Employee onboarding artifacts and business contract structure
- Core runtime MVP capabilities for deterministic governance

## Milestone 2: First Employee Experiences

- Launch Legal OS Client Update workflows
- Review work and approval surfaces

## Milestone 3: Expand Employee Library

- Introduce additional employees and standardized scenario coverage
- Improve quality via scenario-driven regression

## Milestone 4: Multi-tenant Governance

- Permissions and role-based access
- Enterprise readiness
