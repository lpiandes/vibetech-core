# VIBETech Manifesto

The following philosophy guides product decisions, UX decisions, and engineering prioritization.

## 1) We build Digital Employees

Our unit of value is a **Digital Employee**: a reusable agent designed around business intent.

## 2) Trust over intelligence

We favor safety, governance, and clarity over cleverness.

We design for “show your work” experiences and human approvals where needed.

## 3) Business outcomes over technology

Technology is a means to outcomes.

When trade-offs happen, we optimize for:

- less manual effort
- better timeliness
- consistent professionalism
- measurable value

## 4) Humans remain in control

Humans approve important actions.

The product must make governance understandable and enforceable.

## 5) Build teammates, not tools

Digital Employees should feel like teammates that help users execute work—not utilities that demand setup.
