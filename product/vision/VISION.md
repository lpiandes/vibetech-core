# Company Vision

VIBETech is building an AI Operating System for SMBs powered by reusable **Digital Employees**.

We believe small and growing businesses deserve enterprise-grade capabilities without enterprise-grade complexity.

## Product Vision

Our product vision is to make business operations more consistent, faster, and more governed by:

- Turning expert-driven workflows into reusable Digital Employees
- Providing a human-controlled lifecycle for high-impact actions
- Integrating with existing business tools through clear provider boundaries

## Long-term direction

We will scale from “one employee that drafts updates” to a library of reusable employees that can serve many departments and industries.

We will keep core architecture stable so employees remain portable as vendors and infrastructure evolve.

## Target customers

- Law firms with high volume case management
- Financial advisors who manage ongoing client communication
- Manufacturing, construction, and real-estate operators with repeating operational workflows
- Auto dealerships who need consistent follow-up and communication

## Why Digital Employees exist

Digital Employees exist to:

- Preserve business intent as reusable artifacts
- Make workflows repeatable and measurable
- Reduce manual work while keeping humans in control
