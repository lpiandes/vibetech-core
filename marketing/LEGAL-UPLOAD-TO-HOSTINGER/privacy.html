Effective date: July 31, 2026 · Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VibeTech Development (“VibeTech,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information across our business and products. This document is a business-facing policy draft — have counsel review it for your jurisdiction before relying on it as final legal advice.

3. Roles: controller vs processor

  • When you browse our marketing site or create a VibeTech account, we generally act as a controller of that account and inquiry data.
  • When a customer business uses the AI Operating System or Social Checker to process their own leads, employees’ work, or search subjects, that business is typically the controller of that customer/content data, and VibeTech acts as a processor / service provider under their instructions (and our Terms).
  • End customers of our clients should contact that business for rights requests about lead/customer records; we will assist the business as required by contract and law.

6. Text messaging (SMS/MMS), A2P / 10DLC & TCPA

Texting is offered through Twilio (or successors) using numbers associated with the customer’s business brand. Our business customers are responsible for lawful consent, disclosures, and content. VibeTech provides tooling and may assist with registration.

  • Consent / opt-in: Marketing texts require prior express written consent where required by US law; informational texts require appropriate prior express consent. Consent may be collected via website forms, intake, keyword, or in-person disclosures matching the registered message flow.
  • Opt-out: Recipients can text STOP (or equivalent). Opt-outs must be honored promptly; further marketing texts must cease.
  • Help: Recipients can text HELP for assistance information.
  • Frequency: Varies by the customer’s workflows (e.g., lead follow-up, reminders). Message and data rates may apply.
  • Carrier registration: Brand and campaign details (legal entity, EIN, address, sample messages, this Privacy Policy URL, and Terms URL) may be submitted to The Campaign Registry / carriers. Approval is not guaranteed and US delivery may be limited until approved.
  • Platform defaults: Sample STOP/HELP language may be suggested in-product; customers must ensure their public-facing disclosures match actual practice.

7. Voice calling

Voice features (including Knowledge-backed AI receptionists) use Twilio Voice or similar. Call audio/transcripts may be processed to fulfill configured intents. Outbound customer calls may require owner GRANT. Customers must comply with telemarketing, recording-consent, and do-not-call rules in their jurisdictions.

8. Email
