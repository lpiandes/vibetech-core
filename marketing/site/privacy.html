Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026 · Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VibeTech Development (“VibeTech,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our websites, the AI Operating System at app.vtechdevelopment.com, Social Checker at social.vtechdevelopment.com, and related consulting or managed services. This policy is written to support carrier A2P / 10DLC messaging compliance and transparent business practices. It is not a substitute for legal advice.

1. Who we are

VibeTech Development provides business consulting, managed AI services, an AI operating system for businesses, messaging and voice integrations, advertising/lead intake connections, workflow automations, and Social Checker (public-web social discovery tools). Contact: support@vtechdevelopment.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, password (hashed), company/organization name, role, invitation tokens, organization codes.
  • Business profile: legal business name, address, EIN/tax ID (for SMS brand registration), industry, websites, privacy/terms URLs you provide.
  • Customer / lead data you process: names, emails, phone numbers, form answers, Meta Lead Ad submissions, notes, pipeline/work records — as your business’s customer data.
  • Communications: emails, SMS/MMS, voice call metadata and recordings where enabled, approval drafts, automation logs.
  • Integration credentials: OAuth tokens and API credentials for Google, Meta, Twilio, and other connectors you authorize (stored encrypted where applicable).
  • Social Checker queries: search names and resulting public-web discovery summaries you run.
  • Usage & device: IP address, browser type, pages viewed, approximate location, cookies/local storage for sessions and product tours.
  • Billing & commercial: package selections, invoices handled outside or via payment processors as applicable.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, secure, and improve the AI Operating System, Social Checker, and consulting services.
  • Send transactional email (invites, ops handoffs, receipts) and, when you configure channels, customer SMS/email/voice on your behalf subject to your approvals and policies.
  • Register messaging brands/campaigns with carriers (A2P / 10DLC) using the business details you submit.
  • Ingest leads (Meta, website forms, etc.), run automations, and generate AI drafts that may require your GRANT before customer send.
  • Generate Social Checker reports from publicly available web sources; not a consumer credit report under FCRA.
  • Detect abuse, enforce Terms, comply with law, and communicate product updates.

4. Text messaging (SMS/MMS) & A2P / TCPA

If you enable texting, messages are sent through Twilio (or a successor provider) using numbers provisioned for your business. You are responsible for obtaining prior express consent (or prior express written consent where required) from recipients before marketing texts, and for honoring opt-outs.

  • Opt-in: We expect consent via your website forms, intake flows, in-person agreements, or keyword opt-in as disclosed in your message flow.
  • Opt-out: Recipients may reply STOP (or equivalent) to end messaging; you and our systems must process opt-outs promptly.
  • Help: Recipients may reply HELP for assistance information.
  • Message frequency & rates: Frequency varies by your workflows; message and data rates may apply.
  • No sale of phone numbers: We do not sell phone numbers obtained for messaging to third-party marketers.

Brand registration may require your legal name, address, EIN, sample messages, and links to this Privacy Policy and our Terms. Carrier approval is not guaranteed and may take time.

5. Voice calling

Voice features (including AI receptionist) use Twilio Voice or similar. Call content may be processed to fulfill intents you configure. Outbound customer calls may remain approval-gated. You must comply with applicable telemarketing and recording-consent laws.

6. Email

Transactional and customer emails may be sent via Resend, Google (connected inbox), or SMTP. Marketing emails require lawful basis/consent in your jurisdiction. Unsubscribe and preference mechanisms must be respected where required.

7. Meta Lead Ads & advertising connectors

When you (or VibeTech ops on your behalf) connect a Facebook/Instagram Page, lead form submissions are received via Meta webhooks and stored as People/Work records. We process this data to run your intake automations. You remain the controller of lead data for your business; Meta’s policies also apply.

8. Automations & AI processing

Automations may create drafts, update pipelines, or queue messages. AI models (e.g., OpenAI) may process business knowledge and conversation context to generate responses. Customer-facing sends may require explicit approval (“GRANT”) depending on your configuration. Do not upload special-category data unless you have a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

9. Social Checker

Social Checker searches publicly available web sources (e.g., via search/scraping providers) to produce informational summaries. It is not a consumer report for employment, credit, insurance, or housing eligibility under the FCRA or similar laws. You must use it lawfully and only for permitted purposes. Do not treat results as verified identity or background-check determinations.

10. Cookies & local storage

We use essential cookies/session storage for authentication, security, and product state (including tutorials and builder progress). Analytics cookies, if used, will be disclosed and controlled where required by law.

11. How we share information

We share data with:

  • Service providers / subprocessors: hosting (e.g., Vercel), databases (e.g., Neon/Postgres), email (Resend), SMS/voice (Twilio), AI (OpenAI), search/scraping (e.g., Serper, ScrapingBee), Meta, Google, DNS/email hosting (e.g., Hostinger).
  • Your team: users you invite to your organization.
  • Legal & safety: when required by law, to protect rights, or prevent abuse.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.

We do not sell personal information as “sale” is commonly defined under CCPA/CPRA for money, and we do not share phone numbers for third-party SMS marketing.

12. Retention

We retain account and business data while your account is active and as needed for legal, security, and operational purposes. Messaging logs, leads, and automation history may be retained per your workspace configuration and our backup schedules. You may request deletion subject to legal holds and carrier/regulatory requirements.

13. Security

We use industry-standard measures including encryption in transit, access controls, and encrypted storage of sensitive credentials where implemented. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

14. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict processing of personal data, and to opt out of certain processing. Contact support@vtechdevelopment.com. If we process data on behalf of a business customer, we may refer your request to that business.
