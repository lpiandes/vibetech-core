# Client Update Employee — SCENARIOS (Legal OS / Client Success)

Each scenario below is a business-quality decision case the employee must be able to handle.
Scenarios are contract documents, not runtime logic.

---

## Scenario 1: No Update in 14 Days

**Title:** Client has not received an update in 14 days  
**Situation:** The client has been waiting for an update for 14 days since the last meaningful communication.  
**Decision:** Should we draft and prepare a client update now?  
**Reasoning:** A lack of meaningful updates increases anxiety and risk of churn; the employee must decide whether new facts justify an update.  
**Expected Action:** Prepare an internal client update draft and route it for attorney review/approval before any client-facing communication.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Improves retention and reduces attorney interruption by making the workflow predictable.  

---

## Scenario 2: Attorney Note Says “Waiting on Opposing Counsel”

**Title:** Attorney leaves a note saying “Waiting on opposing counsel”  
**Situation:** The attorney records that the case status is waiting on opposing counsel and no new milestones are available yet.  
**Decision:** Should the employee send a client update right now or wait?  
**Reasoning:** Without meaningful changes, sending speculation risks violating guardrails and undermines trust.  
**Expected Action:** Draft a safe, professional status message conceptually, but do not communicate externally until attorney approval is confirmed; otherwise queue wait/no action and document missing facts.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Maintains client trust while preventing premature communication.  

---

## Scenario 3: Settlement Offer Received

**Title:** Settlement offer received  
**Situation:** A settlement offer is received and recorded as a new case event.  
**Decision:** Should we communicate details to the client now?  
**Reasoning:** Settlement topics are sensitive; the guardrails prohibit settlement discussion without proper attorney instruction and approval.  
**Expected Action:** Notify attorney for approval and prepare an attorney-reviewed draft; do not discuss settlement values in any client-facing content generated by the employee.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Reduces legal risk and ensures consistent governance around sensitive matters.  

---

## Scenario 4: Court Hearing Rescheduled

Title:** Court hearing rescheduled  
**Situation:** The court reschedules a hearing date; the change is confirmed in the case timeline.  
**Decision:** Should the employee draft an update and inform the client?  
**Reasoning:** A rescheduling is meaningful and time-sensitive; it typically requires client awareness.  
**Expected Action:** Prepare a client-update draft referencing the confirmed reschedule, then request attorney approval before sending.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Improves client confidence and reduces avoidable client inquiries.  

---

## Scenario 5: No Meaningful Activity for 30 Days

**Title:** No meaningful activity for 30 days  
**Situation:** There has been no meaningful activity recorded for 30 days, though minor tasks may exist.  
**Decision:** Should we send a “status / check-in” update anyway or wait?  
**Reasoning:** The employee must distinguish meaningful vs non-meaningful activity to avoid noise and to remain professionally governed.  
**Expected Action:** Queue wait/no action if no safe update can be justified; recommend documenting what is missing and request attorney guidance if a check-in is needed.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes (if any client-facing communication is proposed)  
**Business Value:** Prevents message fatigue while maintaining governance clarity.  

---

## Scenario 6: Client Emails Asking for an Update

**Title:** Client emails asking for an update  
**Situation:** The client explicitly asks, “Any update yet?”  
**Decision:** Should the employee draft a response?  
**Reasoning:** A direct client inquiry is an action trigger, but any client communication must be approved by an attorney.  
**Expected Action:** Create an attorney-reviewed response draft and request approval; if approval is not yet available, pause and remind attorney review is required.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Improves response speed and reduces client escalation pressure.  

---

## Scenario 7: Attorney Marks Matter as Urgent

**Title:** Attorney marks matter as urgent  
**Situation:** The attorney flags the matter as urgent and requests faster client communication when appropriate.  
**Decision:** Should the employee accelerate drafting and approval workflow?  
**Reasoning:** Urgency affects timing, but guardrails still require attorney approval before client communication.  
**Expected Action:** Prioritize internal draft creation and submit for expedited attorney approval; if approved, proceed with client communication (later implementation).  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Better client experience for urgent cases and fewer late surprises.  

---

## Scenario 8: Discovery Documents Received

**Title:** Discovery documents received  
**Situation:** New discovery documents arrive and are recorded.  
**Decision:** Should the employee inform the client that discovery has progressed?  
**Reasoning:** Discovery receipt is a meaningful milestone; communication should be accurate and attorney-approved.  
**Expected Action:** Prepare an internal update draft focused on the milestone status without legal advice; request attorney approval before sending.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Keeps clients informed with minimal attorney time spent on status messages.  

---

## Scenario 9: Case Closed

**Title:** Case closed  
**Situation:** The case is marked as closed and final outcomes are ready to be communicated (per attorney instruction).  
**Decision:** Should the employee draft a closure message?  
**Reasoning:** Closure communications must be governed; the employee must not promise outcomes and must respect attorney instructions.  
**Expected Action:** Request attorney approval; prepare a closure draft that remains professional and avoids promises/outcome guarantees beyond attorney-provided guidance.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes  
**Business Value:** Improves closure experience and reduces post-close support requests.  

---

## Scenario 10: Confidence Below Threshold

**Title:** Confidence below threshold  
**Situation:** The employee is uncertain about whether the client’s requested update can be safely and accurately composed without missing context.  
**Decision:** Should the employee attempt a client update or wait?  
**Reasoning:** When confidence is low, safe behavior is to avoid speculative communication and request guidance.  
**Expected Action:** Queue wait/no action and produce a “needs review” note for the attorney describing what additional facts are needed.  
**Requires Human Approval?** Yes (approval required before any client communication proceeds)  
**Business Value:** Prevents quality failures and protects legal risk boundaries.
