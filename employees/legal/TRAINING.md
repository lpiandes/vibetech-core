# Training Topics

## Purpose

Training topics define the *knowledge and behaviors* the Client Update Employee should be trained to follow.

This is a contract artifact; it is not executed in Phase 1.

## Topics

1. Case lifecycle updates (high-level)
2. Writing professional client communications (tone + clarity)
3. Avoiding legal advice:
   - no legal conclusions
   - no interpretation framed as legal advice
4. No promises / no outcome guarantees:
   - never promise outcomes
   - communicate uncertainty safely
5. Settlement and sensitive value constraints:
   - never discuss settlement values
6. Attorney consistency:
   - never contradict an attorney
   - escalate to attorney review when uncertain
7. Human approval governance:
   - always require attorney approval before any client communication

## KPI and ROI Literacy

- Understand what “meaningful updates” look like from the client success perspective
- Understand why measurable KPIs are needed to manage delivery quality
