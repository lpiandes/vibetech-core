Short permanent rules:

1. VIBETech’s **sellable product** is managed **Revenue Follow-Through** for B2B service businesses — not seats, CRM modules, AI employees, or a package catalog. The AI Operating System is the **engine**, not the lead SKU.
2. VIBETech owns opportunity detection, acknowledgement, follow-up coordination, scheduling admin, CRM updates, proposal monitoring, handoff prep, and SLA/exception reporting. The customer owns sales conversations, out-of-policy pricing, relationship judgment, negotiation, final commitments, and delivery.
3. The owner supervises decisions and exceptions; VIBETech operates the contracted function; humans approve when required; AI executes within earned autonomy.
4. Ask VIBETech is a grounded command interface — not an empty general-purpose chat product.
5. Everything important is explainable and evidence-backed; Company Rules / Business Memory are the source of truth. Connected is never proven; outcomes without proof are incomplete.
6. One product surface for the managed function — primary IA: Today · Decisions · Outcomes · Company Rules. Never parallel Homes, Builders, or ways to the same goal.
7. Architect / Builder discovers and compiles the Operating Contract VIBETech will execute — not a “customized workspace” tour.
8. Mission Control, DNA, Assembly, Pipeline, Launch, Builder are engineering terms — never owner language.
9. Extend locked architecture; never fork it for UI convenience. Freeze work that fights RFT depth (see DEVELOPMENT_FREEZE.md).
10. If it exposes internal machinery or sells modules instead of a proven operating result, it is the wrong direction.
11. Every plan ships production-grade: reusable functions/files, existing Tailwind/`Button` styling, integrated with the rest of the system, and tested before DONE (`ENGINEERING_SHIP_BAR.md`).
