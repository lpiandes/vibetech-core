# IndustryTemplates.md

## Definition

Industry templates provide starter configuration so owners can begin with clarity.
Templates specify:
- recommended first Digital Employees
- recommended Company Data record types
- recommended Company Knowledge categories
- optional integrations
- common approval rules

Templates are guidance, not constraints:
- owners can adjust after launch
- employees and runtime remain stable because contracts are stable

## Template: Property Management

Recommended first employees:
- Property Interest Coordinator (new property inquiries → response drafts + review task)
- Property Appointment Coordinator (optional future)

Recommended Company Data types:
- Properties
- Buyers
- Owners (optional)
- Inquiries

Recommended Company Knowledge categories:
- response policy for new inquiries
- walkthrough / scheduling scripts
- HOA and timeline guidance scripts

Optional integrations:
- Website intake
- Email delivery
- Calendar
- CRM (optional)

Common approval rules:
- require governance review for all buyer-facing drafts when confidence is not yet sufficient
- require human sign-off for urgent inquiries
